    How to play

    1. Each player chooses a character.
    2. Take turns to roll the dice.
    3. Move forward the number on the dice.
    4. Follow the instructions on your space.
    5. Answer English questions to win stars. A wrong answer sends you back 3 spaces!
    6. The first player to reach FINISH wins!
